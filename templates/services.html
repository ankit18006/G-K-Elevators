Expert diagnosis and setup of elevator controller PCBs and motherboards. We work with all major brands and can source replacement boards for legacy systems.

Precision installation and calibration of all elevator sensors — from landing zone sensors to overload detectors. Accurate sensor setup is critical to safe elevator operation.

  • Annual sensor recalibration
